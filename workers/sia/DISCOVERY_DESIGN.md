# Autonomous discovery design

The discovery provider searches broadly for legitimate suppliers, wholesalers, manufacturers, and distributors. Each candidate preserves its source URL, discovery method, timestamp, and identity evidence. Domains/entities are normalized and deduplicated.

Discovery does not reject a supplier merely because one marketplace is unsuitable. It makes no commercial commitments. Uncertain identity remains uncertain. Multiple independent discovery channels should be used so that one directory or search source is not a single point of failure.

Discovery favors breadth; later viability and opportunity layers provide precision.
