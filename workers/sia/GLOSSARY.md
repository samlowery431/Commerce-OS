# Glossary

- Candidate: discovered supplier identity hypothesis.
- Viability: supplier-level ability to participate in Commerce OS at all.
- Capability: operational feature the supplier supports.
- Catalog admission: usability decision at supplier/category/brand/product scope.
- Possible use: channel × business model × fulfillment model combination.
- Opportunity: feasible use with admissible supply and measured economics.
- Activation: controlled transition from evaluated opportunity to operating integration.
