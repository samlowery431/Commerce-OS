# Quality gates

- Discovery gate: candidate has source provenance and a resolvable supplier identity hypothesis.
- Viability gate: all mandatory supplier-level requirements are evidenced true.
- Catalog gate: usable scope is admitted with sufficient identity/provenance.
- Possible-use gate: channel/model/fulfillment combination is feasible or conditional with explicit constraints.
- Ranking gate: economics are sufficiently measured for comparison.
- Activation gate: required permissions, budgets, and operational integrations are satisfied.
