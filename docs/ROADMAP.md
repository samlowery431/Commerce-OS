# Commerce OS SIA roadmap

## Stage 0 — control plane

Versioned node API, discovery-run lifecycle, candidate persistence/deduplication, CI, security boundary.

## Stage 1 — autonomous discovery

Multiple supplier-discovery channels, provenance capture, domain/entity deduplication, breadth-first candidate acquisition.

## Stage 2 — supplier viability

Marketplace-agnostic evidence acquisition for identity, reseller permission, lawful usable catalog, and IP/counterfeit risk.

## Stage 3 — capabilities and catalog

Fulfillment/ordering/tracking/returns interfaces, catalog acquisition, product normalization, catalog admission.

## Stage 4 — possible uses

Evaluate supplier/product combinations against each sales channel, business model, and fulfillment model without discarding the underlying supplier.

## Stage 5 — economics and ranking

Measure expected contribution, operational burden, risk, capacity, and opportunity cost within feasible uses.

## Stage 6 — activation

Controlled onboarding/integration and autonomous operation subject to explicit financial/permission boundaries.
