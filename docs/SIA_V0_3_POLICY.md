# SIA V0.3 policy boundary

Supplier viability is evaluated independently of any particular sales channel.

Mandatory supplier-level questions are limited to whether the business identity is established, a reseller relationship is permitted, some lawful usable catalog exists, and IP/counterfeit risk is acceptable. Unknown mandatory evidence blocks a VIABLE decision but does not discard the supplier.

Capabilities, catalog admission, possible uses, channel compatibility, economics, ranking, and activation are downstream layers. A channel prohibition removes that use, not the supplier, unless the evidence establishes a supplier-level prohibition on resale or another supplier-level hard failure.
